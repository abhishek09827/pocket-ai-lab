⚡ The Energy Inversion (Tokens per Watt)
While the GTX 1650 is 4.4x faster in token throughput, it burns ~50 Watts. The MediaTek Dimensity 920 operates at just ~4 Watts, delivering 2.66 Tokens/Watt compared to the GPU's 0.92 Tokens/Watt. For battery-powered continuous edge inference, mobile CPUs are 2.8x more energy-efficient!
🧩 The Embodied Causality Deficit in <3B SLMs
Both qwen2.5:1.5b and llama3.2:1b suffered a 0% success rate on ALFWorld embodied planning. 40% of their attempts failed due to invalid actions (attempting to pick objects before opening containers). Small language models lack physical state transition guarantees without external verification.
💻 Code Fine-Tuning as an Embodied Reasoning Anchor
qwen2.5-coder:3b achieved a 60% success rate on ALFWorld with 0% invalid actions, tripling the general 3B model (20%). Syntax and control-flow training directly reinforce causal state-tracking. However, code fine-tuning introduced a 40% factual hallucination rate on non-code HotpotQA tasks.
🔥 Sustained Thermal Degradation (-18.8%)
Continuous inference on the GTX 1650 pushes the die temperature from 79°C to the 82°C NVIDIA target limit, causing boost clocks to throttle and dropping decode throughput from 79.81 TPS down to 64.78 TPS (-18.83%). True edge benchmarking must account for thermal equilibrium.
📈 $O(N)$ vs $O(N^2)$ Context Scaling & KV-Cache Footprint
Scaling prompt context from 256 to 4,328 tokens on the 4GB GTX 1650 causes TTFT to jump from 1.1s to 21.5s as attention complexity and memory paging mount. The KV-cache expands from 1,435 MB to 1,524 MB (+89 MB footprint) while generation throughput drops from 44.6 TPS to 38.3 TPS.
